Privacy Notice — Chocolate Margin Benchmark

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · DRAFT

Who is responsible

EU Price Index is run by Eduard Arbitman, sole proprietor (Kyiv, Ukraine). Contact: partners@eupriceindex.com.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the survey: country, business type, what you buy, purchase price range and currency, retail price or product link, monthly volume, your email and your consent choices. We do not ask for your name or company name.

Why we use it

  • To calculate anonymous benchmarks (medians and quartiles). Legal basis: your consent.
  • To email you the free monthly report — only if you ticked that box. Legal basis: your consent.

What is published

  • Only medians and quartiles, never a single business's numbers.
  • A group is shown only with 5 or more participants; otherwise EU-wide figures only.
  • Purchase prices are shown with a one-month delay.

Where it is stored

Answers are collected with Tally (tally.so, EU-based form service) and kept in a private, access-restricted storage. They are never placed in a public repository, never sold and never shared with third parties for marketing.

How long

As long as you take part in the benchmark. If you withdraw, we delete your answers and email within 30 days. Published aggregates cannot be traced back to you and are kept.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent at any time, and unsubscribe from the report with one click. Write to partners@eupriceindex.com. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your EU country.
