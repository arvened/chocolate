Chocolate Price Index — Poland & Germany

Median online prices for chocolate in euros per kilogram, collected every day from 13 independent online shops (6 in Poland, 7 in Germany; no marketplaces), plus official Eurostat price indices.

Method

Every day a script reads public product pages of the shops (structured data: price, weight, availability). Products are classified by type (dark, milk, white), format (bars and tablets, or bulk packs ≥ 1 kg such as couverture and callets) and Belgian origin. The index is the median €/kg per group. Products without a weight are excluded. Full rules: editorial policy.
